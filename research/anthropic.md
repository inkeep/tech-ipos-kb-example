## Question

Where does Anthropic stand on the path to an IPO, and who funds it?

## Context

In May 2026 Anthropic **overtook OpenAI** to become the most valuable AI startup. Its capital structure leans on two hyperscalers (Amazon, Google) rather than one, and it deliberately multi-sources silicon. Pairs with [OpenAI](/research/openai.md) and feeds the [AI capital web](/research/ai-funding-web.md).

> [!NOTE] Snapshot (mid-2026)
> - **Valuation:** ~$965B (Series H, May 28 2026) — now **#1** among AI startups
> - **Round:** $65B — likely its **last private round** before an IPO
> - **Revenue:** ~$47B run-rate (from ~$30B earlier in 2026 and ~$10B in 2025) — ~80x growth, driven by **Claude Code**
> - **Backers:** Amazon (~$13B total), Google (~$3B, ~14% stake), plus a deep institutional syndicate
> - **IPO:** no filing yet; WSJ reports Anthropic expects a ~130% revenue surge to its **first operating profit**

## Valuation & funding trajectory

| Date | Round | Valuation |
|---|---|---:|
| Feb 2026 | Series G | $380B |
| May 28 2026 | **Series H ($65B)** | **$965B** |

The Series H was led by **Altimeter, Dragoneer, Greenoaks, and Sequoia**, with a broad syndicate (Capital Group, Coatue, D1, GIC, ICONIQ, XN; Baillie Gifford, Blackstone, Brookfield, Fidelity, T. Rowe Price, Temasek, and more) and strategic infra partners **Micron, Samsung, SK hynix**. It included **$15B of previously committed hyperscaler money, $5B from Amazon** ([Series H](/external-sources/anthropic-series-h-65b.md)).

## Revenue

Run-rate revenue **crossed $47B** in May 2026, up from ~$30B earlier in the year and ~$10B in 2025 — growth driven largely by **Claude Code** and enterprise adoption ([source](/external-sources/anthropic-series-h-65b.md)).

> [!WARNING] Comparability caveat
> Anthropic reports revenue from cloud resellers (AWS, Google, Azure) on a **gross basis** (counting total end-customer spend, booking partner payouts as expense), which inflates the top line versus net-reporting peers. Compare run-rate figures across labs with care.

Anthropic runs a **three-platform** compute strategy — Google TPUs, Amazon Trainium, Nvidia GPUs — and the SpaceX/Colossus tie is the bridge that wires SpaceX into the AI web (see [SpaceX](/research/spacex.md)).

## IPO outlook

- **Status:** No public/confidential filing reported yet, but the Series H is widely framed as the **last private round before an IPO**.
- **Tailwinds:** fastest revenue ramp among the frontier labs; a clearer line of sight to operating profit (WSJ) than OpenAI's ~2030 cash-flow-positive estimate.
- **Watch items:** the gross-basis revenue caveat; concentration of compute/funding in Amazon + Google; valuation now ahead of OpenAI despite a smaller consumer footprint.
