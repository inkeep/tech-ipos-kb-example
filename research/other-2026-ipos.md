## Question

The KB profiles three mega-IPOs — [SpaceX](/research/spacex.md), [OpenAI](/research/openai.md), and [Anthropic](/research/anthropic.md). What *else* is going public (or lining up to) in 2026, and how does it relate to the same AI capital web?

## Context

The "big three" are the headliners, not the whole class. As of June 2026 only one large tech name has actually **priced and listed** (Cerebras); the big three have all *filed* but not yet traded. Beneath them sits a broad pipeline — and most of it is AI or AI-adjacent, which is why the [AI capital web](/research/ai-funding-web.md) is the connective tissue of the entire cohort, not just the anchors.

> [!NOTE] Snapshot (June 5, 2026)
> - **Already public:** Cerebras (CBRS) — the biggest *completed* tech IPO of 2026 so far
> - **Filed, not yet trading:** SpaceX, Anthropic, OpenAI (the big three), plus Lime
> - **Expected / under consideration:** Databricks, Stripe, Canva, Cohere, Strava

## Already public in 2026

### Cerebras (CBRS) — the one that has actually listed

| Metric | Value |
|---|---:|
| Priced | $185/share (May 13, 2026) — above range |
| Raised | ~$5.5B (up to ~$6.38B with greenshoe) |
| Day-one pop | ~108% (opened ~$385, closed ~$311) |
| Valuation | ~$56.4B fully diluted at IPO price |
| 2025 revenue | $510M (+76% YoY), net income $237.8M |

The wafer-scale AI chipmaker is the **biggest completed tech IPO of 2026 so far** ([Cerebras IPO](/external-sources/cerebras-ipo-2026.md)). It wires directly into the AI capital web: **OpenAI is a named customer**, alongside G42, MBZUAI, and AWS — but ~86% of revenue is tied to **UAE-linked entities**, the concentration risk that stalled its 2024 attempt under CFIUS review.

> [!CAUTION] Superlative to handle carefully
> Some outlets called Cerebras the "largest US tech IPO since Uber (2019)." By **capital raised** that is contestable — Rivian (2021) raised ~$11.9B vs. Cerebras's ~$5.5B (see [historical tech IPOs](/research/historical-tech-ipos.md)). The well-supported claim is narrower: **biggest *completed* tech IPO of 2026 to date.**

## Why this matters for the capital web

The same hyperscalers and chip firms that anchor the [AI capital web](/research/ai-funding-web.md) — Nvidia, Microsoft, Amazon, Google, Oracle, AMD, Broadcom — are investors, suppliers, *and* customers across this whole class, not just the big three. Cerebras (OpenAI as customer) and Databricks/Cohere (enterprise AI) extend the same web. **CoreWeave** is a related node already in the KB, though it listed in **2025**, not 2026.
