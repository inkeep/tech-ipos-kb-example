## Cerebras IPO — key facts

Cerebras Systems, the wafer-scale AI chipmaker, completed the **first big tech IPO of 2026** and the largest one to actually price and list so far this year (SpaceX / OpenAI / Anthropic have filed but not yet listed).

- **Priced:** May 13, 2026 at **$185/share** — above the expected range
- **Listed:** May 14, 2026 on **Nasdaq**, ticker **CBRS**
- **Shares sold:** 30 million
- **Amount raised:** **~$5.5 billion** (some reports $5.55B; up to ~$6.38B if the underwriters' 4.5M-share greenshoe is exercised)
- **Day-one trading:** opened ~$385, **popped ~108%**, closed ~$311
- **Valuation:** **~$56.4B fully diluted at the $185 IPO price**; ~$66B end-of-day at the close (CNBC cited ~$95B on a different basis)

## Financials (2025)

- Revenue: **$510M** (+76% YoY)
- Net income: **$237.8M** (vs. ~$500M loss in 2024)

## Customer concentration

Key customers: **OpenAI, Group 42 (G42), Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence, and AWS**. Roughly **86% of revenue** is tied to UAE-linked entities — a concentration risk flagged at IPO. Earlier 2024 listing attempts stalled over CFIUS review of the large G42 investment and this revenue concentration.
