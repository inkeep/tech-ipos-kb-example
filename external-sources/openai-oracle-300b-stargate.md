## Source

- **$300 billion** compute purchase from **Oracle** over **five years**, requiring ~**4.5 gigawatts** of capacity; reported to **begin in 2027** (WSJ). The contract was announced in July 2025; the ~$300B value was reported in September 2025.
- Part of **Stargate**, the **$500 billion** AI-infrastructure initiative announced January 2025 by **OpenAI, Oracle, and SoftBank** (with MGX) at the White House — a 10 GW commitment.
- Five new Stargate sites (Wyoming, Pennsylvania, Texas, Michigan, New Mexico) plus the Abilene, TX flagship and CoreWeave projects bring Stargate to **~7 GW** of planned capacity and **>$400B** of investment over three years.
- The Abilene flagship is built/owned by **Crusoe**, operated by Oracle.
- Oracle's remaining performance obligations (RPO) swelled to **$455B** at the end of Q1 FY2026; reporting notes the $300B OpenAI contract is described by management as **non-cancellable**.

## Highlights

- **$300B / 5yr / 4.5 GW** Oracle compute commitment, begins 2027.
- Sits inside **Stargate** ($500B, OpenAI + SoftBank + Oracle + MGX).
- Drove Oracle's RPO to ~$455B and a sharp stock/Ellison-wealth spike.
