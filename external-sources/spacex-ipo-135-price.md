## Source

**The pricing**
- SpaceX is targeting a **fixed offer price of $135 per share** for its IPO roadshow — **not a conventional price range**. Reuters reported the terms June 2–3, 2026.
- **555.6 million shares** offered (all primary), raising **~$75 billion** at an implied **~$1.75 trillion** valuation. ($135 × 555.6M ≈ $75.0B.)
- **Pricing date ~June 11, 2026**; **listing ~June 12, 2026** on **Nasdaq** (and Nasdaq Texas), ticker **SPCX**.
- This is **a fixed-price, all-primary raise rather than a range-led process** — SpaceX bypassed the traditional range-then-narrow bookbuild. Bookbuilding/allocation expected to conclude in the **June 8–10** window ahead of June 11 pricing.
- The **S-1/A amendment (as of June 1)** still left the cover-page **share count and price range blank** — the $135 figure comes from reporting on the roadshow, not yet the effective prospectus.

## Highlights

- **$135/share fixed** (no range), **555.6M shares**, **~$75B raise**, **~$1.75T** valuation.

- Fixed-price/all-primary structure; terms reported by Reuters, unofficial until the pricing notice.
