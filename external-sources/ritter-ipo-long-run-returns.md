> ⚠ Text-extracted key tables from Jay R. Ritter's *Initial Public Offerings: Updated Long-run Statistics* (University of Florida, April 7 2026 edition). Ritter is the standard academic source on IPO long-run performance. The full PDF (the [source_url](https://site.warrington.ufl.edu/ritter/files/IPOs-long-run-returns-on-IPOs.pdf)) has ~30 tables; the four below are what the [average-IPO benchmark lines](/research/post-ipo-performance.md) are built on. 9,253–9,254 operating-company IPOs, 1980–2024, returns through Dec 31 2025; excludes offer price < $5, units, ADRs, REITs, SPACs, closed-end funds, banks/S&Ls.

## The headline: the *average* IPO underperforms the market

> [!IMPORTANT] Mean ≠ typical
> The **mean** IPO return is positive, but it's dragged up by a handful of moonshots (Yahoo!, Tesla, Moderna…). The **median** IPO is *underwater* over 3 and 5 years, and the average IPO underperforms size-matched firms by **~3.3% per year** over its first five years. "Average returns" and "what happens to the typical IPO" are very different questions.

## Table 20-1 — average returns by event year, from the first-day close (1980–2024)

| | 1st yr | 2nd yr | 3rd yr | 4th yr | 5th yr | Geo. mean yrs 1–5 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **IPO firms** | 5.6% | 5.8% | 11.8% | 20.5% | 10.0% | **10.6%/yr** |
| Size-matched | 11.4% | 13.7% | 15.6% | 16.3% | 12.5% | 13.9%/yr |
| Difference | −5.8% | −7.9% | −3.8% | +4.2% | −2.5% | **−3.3%/yr** |

(Per-year returns, equal-weighted, rebalanced annually; not including the first-day return. First six months +4.9%.)

## Buy-and-hold returns, mean vs median (the benchmark anchors)

| Horizon | Mean BHR **from offer** | Median BHR **from offer** |
|---|---:|---:|
| 3-year | **+36.3%** (Table 16f, all 1980–2024) | **−16.6%** (Table 16e, 1975–2021) |
| 5-year | **+57.2%** (Table 16e, 1975–2021) | **−22.1%** (Table 16e, 1975–2021) |

Measured from the first close instead: 3-yr mean BHR +19.1%, 5-yr +37.8% (median −25.7% / −32.0%).

## First-day return (the "pop")

Average first-day return 1980–2024 = **+18.9%** (offer → first close). Tech IPOs +31.2%, non-tech +12.0% (Table 16f/19). It's the *long-run* drift, not the pop, where IPOs lag.

## Distribution of 3-year BHR from offer (Table 16e, all 9,195 IPOs 1975–2021)

- **BHR < −50%:** 35.0% of IPOs · **−50% to 0%:** 21.1% → **~56% of IPOs lose money** over 3 years from the offer.
- 0–50%: 14.7% · 50–100%: 9.9% · 100–200%: 9.6% · 200–500%: 7.4% · >500%: 2.4%. The thin right tail (the moonshots) is what lifts the *mean* above the *median*.
