## Source

**Anthropic to Expand Use of Google Cloud TPUs** — October 23, 2025

- Anthropic gains access to **up to one million** Google **TPUs**; the deal is **worth tens of billions of dollars** and is expected to bring **well over a gigawatt** of compute online in **2026** — Anthropic's largest TPU commitment yet.
- Anthropic runs a **three-platform** compute strategy: Google **TPUs**, Amazon **Trainium**, and Nvidia **GPUs**. AWS remains its primary training partner (Project Rainier).
- **Google's stake:** Google invested ~**$2B in 2023** and ~**$1B in 2025** (~$3B total). **March 2026 court documents revealed Google holds a ~14% stake** in Anthropic.
- **April 2026 expansion:** Anthropic signed an additional deal with **Google and Broadcom** for up to **5 GW** of next-generation TPU capacity starting **2027**.

### Quote

- **Thomas Kurian** (Google Cloud CEO): Anthropic's expansion "reflects the strong price-performance and efficiency its teams have seen with TPUs for several years… including our seventh generation TPU, Ironwood."

## Highlights

- **Up to 1M TPUs**, **>1 GW** in 2026, **"tens of billions"**; **+5 GW** (Google/Broadcom) from 2027.
- Google: ~**$3B** invested, **~14% stake** (per March 2026 court docs).
- Anthropic deliberately multi-sources silicon (TPU + Trainium + GPU).
