## Source

**CoreWeave–OpenAI commitments (2025):**
- Initial contract up to **$11.9 billion** (early 2025), a **$4 billion** expansion (May), and a **$6.5 billion** expansion (September 25, 2025) → **~$22.4 billion** total.

**Nvidia ↔ CoreWeave:**
- Nvidia holds an **~6% equity stake** (worth ~$0.9B in March 2025, ~$1.6B by May).
- In September 2025, Nvidia agreed to **purchase $6.3 billion** of CoreWeave cloud capacity — effectively a **backstop** (Nvidia pays for GPU time not sold to other customers).

**Microsoft ↔ CoreWeave:**
- Microsoft is the **anchor customer**: ~**62% of 2024 revenue** and ~**67% of 2025 revenue**. Management expects that to fall below 50% as OpenAI and Meta contracts ramp.

**Backlog:** CoreWeave reported **$66.8 billion** in remaining performance obligations as of Dec 31, 2025 (4× the start-of-year backlog), anchored by Microsoft, OpenAI, and Meta.

## Highlights

- **$22.4B** OpenAI → CoreWeave (3 deals in 2025).
- Nvidia: ~**6%** stake **+ $6.3B** capacity backstop — a circular-financing node.
- Microsoft anchor customer (~67% of 2025 revenue); CoreWeave RPO **$66.8B**.
