## All-time record IPOs (all sectors, for scale)

Per DealRoom and Wikipedia "List of largest IPOs":

- Saudi Aramco — **$25.6B** (→$29.4B with greenshoe), 2019, oil
- Alibaba — **$21.8B**, 2014, tech
- SoftBank Corp — **$21.3B**, 2018, telecom
- NTT DoCoMo — **$18.1B**, 1998, telecom
- Visa — **$17.9B**, 2008, fintech
- Facebook — **$16.0B**, 2012, tech

Some 2026 trackers already list **SpaceX at the top** of all-time rankings; the reported raise ranges from ~$30B in earlier rankings to **up to ~$75B** in the IPO-filing reporting — the figure is a moving target until pricing.

## Highlights

- The prior **tech** record is **Alibaba ($21.8B, 2014)**; the prior **U.S.-company** tech record is **Facebook ($16.0B, 2012)**.
- SpaceX's ~$75B target would be **~3.4× Alibaba** and dwarf every prior IPO in any sector.
- **Coinbase (2021)** and several others went public via **direct listing** (no capital raised) — excluded from "amount raised" comparisons.
