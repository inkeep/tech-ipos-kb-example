**Anthropic Raises $65B in Series H** — May 28, 2026

- **$65 billion** raised at a **$965 billion** post-money valuation — nearly **triple** the **$380B** Series G valuation from February 2026, and **above OpenAI's** $852B.
- **Run-rate revenue crossed $47 billion** earlier in May (up from ~$30B run-rate earlier in 2026 and ~$10B in 2025 — described as ~80x growth, driven largely by **Claude Code**).
- Includes **$15 billion of previously committed hyperscaler investments**, with **$5 billion from Amazon**.
- Widely characterized as **possibly Anthropic's last private round before going public**. The WSJ reported Anthropic expects a ~**130% revenue surge** to reach its **first operating profit**.

**Investors**
- **Lead:** Altimeter Capital, Dragoneer, Greenoaks, Sequoia Capital.
- **Co-leads:** Capital Group, Coatue, D1 Capital Partners, GIC, ICONIQ, XN.
- **Significant:** Baillie Gifford, Blackstone, Brookfield, D. E. Shaw Ventures, DST Global, Fidelity, General Catalyst, Insight Partners, Jane Street, Lightspeed, MGX, T. Rowe Price, Temasek, and others.
- **Strategic infrastructure partners:** **Micron, Samsung, SK hynix**.

**Compute footprint cited**
- Agreements with **Amazon** (up to 5 GW), **Google + Broadcom** (5 GW of next-gen TPUs), and **SpaceX** (GPU capacity in **Colossus 1 and 2**). Claude is the first frontier model on AWS, Google Cloud, and Microsoft Azure; **AWS remains the primary cloud & training partner**.
- CFO **Krishna Rao**: the funding helps Anthropic "serve the historic demand we are experiencing, stay at the research frontier."

## Highlights

- **$65B @ $965B** (May 28, 2026) — now the **most valuable AI startup**, ahead of OpenAI.
- **>$47B run-rate** revenue; likely **last private round** pre-IPO.
- Compute partners span Amazon, Google/Broadcom, and **SpaceX (Colossus)** — the bridge tying SpaceX into the AI web.
