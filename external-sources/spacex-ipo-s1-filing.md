## Source

**The filing**
- SpaceX filed for an IPO on **May 20, 2026**, ticker **SPCX**, aiming to raise **up to $75 billion** at a valuation of **at least ~$1.75–1.8 trillion** — more than 2× the prior record IPO (Saudi Aramco, $1.7T, 2019).
- **Goldman Sachs** leads; **retail investors earmarked ~30%** of the float. Share sale expected **as early as June 11**, listing the next day.
- Valuation trajectory: **$74B (2021)** → ~$350B (mid-2025 tender) → **~$421/share** (December 2025 tender) → ~$1.75T (IPO target).

**Financials (from the filing)**
- **Revenue $18.7B in 2025** (+33% YoY). **Net loss $4.9B in 2025** (after a $791M profit in 2024; –$4.6B in 2023). Q1 2026: **–$4.3B loss on $4.7B revenue**.
- Total spend $20.7B in 2025: **$12.7B on AI**, $4.2B on Starlink, $3.8B on other space ventures.

**Starlink (the engine)**
- **$11.4B revenue in 2025 (~61% of total)**, **$4.4B operating profit** — the only consistently GAAP-profitable segment.
- **5M subscribers (Feb 2026) → 10M shortly after**; ARPU fell ~18% to **$81/month**.
- Analysts estimate Starlink is ~40–55% of the IPO valuation despite ~65–80% of current revenue (the rest is speculative: Starship, xAI, orbital AI data centers).

**xAI merger & AI pivot**
- February 2026: SpaceX **merged with xAI** at a reported combined valuation of ~**$1.25 trillion** (xAI valued ~$80B in the transaction). *(These figures are reported but not fully reconciled with the ~$1.75T IPO target; treat as provisional.)* SpaceX pitches a combined "space and AI powerhouse," citing a **$28.5 trillion** total addressable market.

## Highlights

- **SPCX**, ~**$1.75T** target, **up to $75B** raise, listing ~**June 11–12, 2026** — likely the **largest IPO ever**.
- **Starlink**: $11.4B rev / $4.4B op profit / ~10M subs — the cash engine.
- Company-wide **$4.9B net loss (2025)**, heavy AI spend; **xAI merged in** (Feb 2026).
