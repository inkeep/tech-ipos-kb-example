## Source

**AMD and OpenAI Announce Strategic Partnership to Deploy 6 Gigawatts of AMD GPUs** — October 6, 2025

- OpenAI will deploy **6 gigawatts** of AMD **Instinct** GPUs over multiple years and hardware generations, beginning with an initial **1 GW** rollout in the **second half of 2026** on AMD's next-gen **Instinct MI450**.
- **Warrant:** AMD issued OpenAI a **warrant for up to 160 million shares** of AMD common stock at an exercise price of **$0.01 per share**, vesting in tranches as deployment scales from 1 GW to 6 GW and as AMD hits share-price targets (final tranche tied to a **$600** share price).
- **Stake:** Full exercise would give OpenAI roughly **10% ownership** of AMD.
- Market reaction: AMD shares rose ~**23.7%** on the news. At the $600 target, 160M shares would be worth ~**$96B**.
- Sam Altman framed the deal as **incremental** to (not a replacement for) Nvidia purchases: "The world needs much more compute."

## Highlights

- **6 GW** AMD Instinct GPUs; first 1 GW H2 2026 (MI450).
- **160M-share warrant @ $0.01** → potential **~10% AMD stake** for OpenAI (a GPU-for-equity structure).
- AMD later struck a structurally identical 6 GW / ~10% deal with Meta.
