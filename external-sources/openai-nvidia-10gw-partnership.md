## Source

**OpenAI and NVIDIA Announce Strategic Partnership to Deploy 10 Gigawatts of NVIDIA Systems** — September 22, 2025

- **Investment:** NVIDIA intends to invest **up to $100 billion** in OpenAI, deployed **progressively as each gigawatt** comes online.
- **Scale:** At least **10 gigawatts** of NVIDIA AI data-center systems, representing **millions of GPUs**.
- **Timeline:** First gigawatt targeted for the **second half of 2026**, on the NVIDIA **Vera Rubin** platform.
- The two companies signed a **letter of intent**; OpenAI will treat NVIDIA as a **preferred strategic compute and networking partner**, co-optimizing model and infrastructure software with NVIDIA hardware.
- Collaborators referenced: Microsoft, Oracle, SoftBank, and Stargate partners.
- OpenAI cited **over 700 million weekly active users** at the time of the announcement.

### Quotes

- **Jensen Huang** (NVIDIA founder/CEO): the companies have "pushed each other for a decade"; called this "the next leap forward."
- **Sam Altman** (OpenAI cofounder/CEO): "Everything starts with compute"; compute infrastructure "will be the basis for the economy of the future."
- **Greg Brockman** (OpenAI cofounder/president): "We've been working closely with NVIDIA since the early days of OpenAI."

## Highlights

- Up to **$100B** Nvidia → OpenAI investment (progressive, per-GW).
- **10 GW** / millions of GPUs; first GW H2 2026 on Vera Rubin.
- Non-binding **letter of intent** stage at announcement; non-exclusive (OpenAI also uses AMD, Google TPUs, and its own chips).
