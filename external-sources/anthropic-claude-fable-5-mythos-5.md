## Extracted source notes

Anthropic announced Claude Fable 5 and Claude Mythos 5 on June 9, 2026. The post describes Fable 5 as a Mythos-class model made safe for general use and says its capabilities exceed any Anthropic model previously made generally available. The post says Fable 5 is state of the art on nearly all tested benchmarks and is especially strong when tasks are long and complex.

Anthropic says Fable 5 and Mythos 5 can work autonomously for longer than previous Claude models. The software-engineering example is Stripe: during early testing, Stripe reported that Fable 5 completed a codebase-wide migration in a 50-million-line Ruby codebase in one day, compared with more than two months for a human team by hand.

The knowledge-work examples include Hebbia's finance benchmark and IMC's trading-analysis evaluations. Anthropic says Fable 5 scored highest on Hebbia's senior-level finance reasoning benchmark and that IMC found it strong across factual lookup, conceptual reasoning, root-cause analysis, and expected-value analysis.

The vision examples include extracting precise numbers from detailed scientific figures, rebuilding a web app's source code from screenshots alone, and completing Pokemon FireRed with only a minimal vision-only harness. Anthropic contrasts this with earlier Claude models that needed more scaffolding to play the game.

The memory and long-context examples include Slay the Spire, where persistent file-based memory improved Fable 5 more than it improved Opus 4.8. Anthropic also lists demonstration artifacts: a solar-system simulation that predicts eclipses, autonomous Factorio play, a browser-based CAD editor and 3D-printable model, and a fluid simulation synchronized to an EDM remix generated by code.

Anthropic separates Fable 5 from Mythos 5 on safeguards. It says Mythos 5 uses the same underlying model as Fable 5 with some safeguards lifted for a limited set of cyberdefenders, infrastructure providers, and planned trusted life-sciences users. Public Fable 5 falls back to Claude Opus 4.8 for some cybersecurity, biology and chemistry, and distillation requests. Anthropic says early data showed more than 95% of Fable sessions had no fallback.

Anthropic says Mythos-class models show both promise and risk in biology. The post describes Mythos 5 accelerating internal drug-design work, producing molecular-biology hypotheses, and conducting genomics research. Those examples are attached to Mythos 5 or trusted-access paths, not to unrestricted public Fable 5.

The post lists pricing at $10 per million input tokens and $50 per million output tokens. It says developers can use the `claude-fable-5` model via the Claude API, and that subscription-plan access began with a staged rollout window.
