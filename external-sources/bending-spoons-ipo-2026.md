## Extracted source notes

Axios reported on June 8, 2026 that Bending Spoons filed for a U.S. IPO that could value the company at around **$20B**. The company is an Italian software holding company that owns AOL, Eventbrite, and Vimeo, and the report said it booked **$601M of Q1 revenue**. Goldman Sachs, JPMorgan, and Allen & Co. were named as lead bookrunners.
