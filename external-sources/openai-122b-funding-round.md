## Source

**The round**

- **March 31, 2026:** OpenAI closed the **largest private funding round in history** at an **$852 billion** post-money valuation, totaling **$122 billion** of committed capital (up from a $110B figure announced in February at a $730B pre-money valuation).
- **Strategic anchors:** **Amazon** agreed to invest **up to $50 billion**, **Nvidia $30 billion**, **SoftBank $30 billion**. SoftBank **co-led** alongside **a16z, D. E. Shaw Ventures, MGX, TPG**, and accounts advised by **T. Rowe Price**.
- For the first time, OpenAI extended participation via bank channels and **raised $3 billion from individual investors**.

**Revenue & usage**

- **$13.1 billion** booked revenue in 2025; generating **\~$2 billion per month** at announcement. Still burning cash and not yet profitable.
- Annualized run-rate ~~**$20B** at end of 2025 (CFO **Sarah Friar**: $6B in 2024, $2B in 2023) → Sacra estimates \*\*~~$25B annualized by February 2026\*\* (excludes Microsoft licensing & one-time deals).
- **900M+ weekly active** ChatGPT users; **50M+ paying** subscribers; API volume **>15B tokens/minute**. Enterprise is **>40% of revenue**, on track for parity with consumer by end of 2026.

**IPO**

- OpenAI has **filed confidentially** with the SEC, targeting the **second half of 2026**, with a **$1 trillion** listing goal by 2027.
- Sacra projects OpenAI is **not cash-flow positive until \~2030**.
- Skeptic note: NYU's **Aswath Damodaran** argues the headline assumes revenue growth no tech company has sustained at this scale and that conditional/compute-linked tranches overstate the cash injection.

## Highlights

- **$122B raised @ $852B** post-money (Mar 31, 2026); confidential SEC filing, H2 2026 target, $1T goal by 2027.
- **$13.1B** booked 2025 rev; \~$25B annualized by Feb 2026; not CF-positive until \~2030.
- Notable: **Amazon (up to $50B)** and **Nvidia ($30B)** are now equity investors, not just compute counterparties.
