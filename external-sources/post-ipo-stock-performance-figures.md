## Highlights

- **Snowflake** is the only one of the three that spent its entire first ~2 years *above* its offer price — but a public investor who bought at the day-1 close (~$254) was underwater for most of the period.
- **Uber** took ~4.5 years to durably clear its $45 offer; the 2023 +142% year is what got it there.
- **Rivian** is the cautionary tale: −82% in 2022 alone, and still ~75–80% below its offer price at the 4-year mark.

## Tesla benchmark (2010 IPO — added for scale)

Tesla is **not** part of the 2019–2021 cohort — it's included on the chart as the *generational IPO winner*, for scale. IPO **June 29, 2010 at $17.00**; first-day close **$23.89 (+40.5%)**.
