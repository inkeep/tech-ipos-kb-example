## Source

**Amazon's investment (cumulative $8B, 2023–2024):** $1.25B (Sep 2023) + $2.75B (early 2024) + $1.3B + a final $4B (late 2024), establishing **AWS as Anthropic's primary training partner and cloud provider**. Amazon remains a **minority** investor (exact % not disclosed at the time; later Google filings put Google at ~14% for comparison).

**April 2026 expansion:** Amazon announced an additional **$5 billion** investment, with **up to $20 billion more** in the future. Concurrently, Anthropic pledged **more than $100 billion over ten years** to AWS, securing **up to 5 GW** of new capacity (Graviton + Trainium2 through Trainium4, with options on future custom silicon).

**Project Rainier:** AWS activated a compute cluster of **nearly 500,000 Trainium2 chips** across multiple US data centers to train and serve Claude (first announced at re:Invent late 2024; completed 2025).

## Highlights

- Amazon: **$8B** invested (2023–24) **+ $5B** (Apr 2026, up to **+$20B**) — Anthropic's largest backer.
- Anthropic → AWS: **$100B+ / 10yr**, up to **5 GW** (Trainium2→Trainium4).
- **Project Rainier**: ~**500k Trainium2** chips — a vertically integrated Amazon↔Anthropic compute loop.
