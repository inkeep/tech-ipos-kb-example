## Source

**OpenAI and Broadcom Announce Strategic Collaboration to Deploy 10 Gigawatts of OpenAI-Designed AI Accelerators** — October 13, 2025

- Collaboration for **10 gigawatts** of **custom AI accelerators**. **OpenAI designs** the accelerators and systems; **Broadcom** co-develops and deploys them, scaled with Broadcom's **Ethernet** networking stack.
- **Timeline:** racks targeted to start deploying in the **second half of 2026**, completing by **end of 2029**.
- First public confirmation of an ~18-month working relationship; a term sheet was signed.
- Financial terms were **not disclosed**; the **Financial Times estimated $350–500 billion** of cost to OpenAI.
- Reporting notes Arm is separately developing a custom CPU for the Broadcom-built accelerator (potentially worth billions to SoftBank); designs span 3nm and 2nm.

### Quotes

- **Sam Altman** (OpenAI CEO): "Partnering with Broadcom is a critical step in building the infrastructure needed to unlock AI's potential…"
- **Hock Tan** (Broadcom CEO): the collaboration "signifies a pivotal moment in the pursuit of artificial general intelligence."

## Highlights

- **10 GW** OpenAI-**designed** custom silicon, built with Broadcom (Ethernet-scaled).
- FT est. **$350–500B**; deploys H2 2026 → 2029.
- Signals OpenAI's vertical move into its own chips (alongside Nvidia/AMD GPUs).
